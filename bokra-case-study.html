01 · THE CHALLENGE

Changing how people perceived the product.

When I joined Bokra, one of the biggest challenges was not simply promoting the app. It was changing the assumption that financial saving and wealth-building platforms are designed only for people with large amounts of money.

Many potential users could afford to save small amounts, but they did not naturally see themselves as the audience for this type of product. The communication needed to show that Bokra was built for ordinary people who wanted to put aside whatever they could and allow it to grow over time through Sharia-compliant financial products.

02 · THE INSIGHT

People trust brands that reflect their own reality.

Instead of presenting polished corporate models or luxury lifestyles, I wanted every potential customer to recognize someone familiar in Bokra’s communication.

“When people see someone like them using the product, the brand starts to feel like it was built for them.”

03 · THE SOLUTION

A character system built around real Egyptian lives.

I developed a visual direction centered on realistic Egyptian characters representing different professions, ages, and social backgrounds.

Using AI as a creative production tool, I built an extensive character library while carefully directing clothing, expressions, occupations, and visual details to make every person feel authentic and culturally familiar.

Each character communicated the same underlying idea: people just like you are already saving with Bokra. This shifted the focus away from financial status and toward a more accessible behavior—starting small, saving consistently, and growing money over time.

04 · CREATIVE DIRECTION

Aspiration through representation, not luxury.

The campaign avoided the traditional financial visual language of wealth, premium lifestyles, and exclusive investors. Instead, it celebrated ordinary Egyptians from a wide range of professions.

The group composition created a sense of scale and collective participation, while the familiar faces made the brand feel more inclusive, trustworthy, and socially relevant.

By seeing someone who looked like them, worked like them, or managed money in a similar way, each audience segment could understand that Bokra was designed to serve their goals.

THE DESIGN GOAL

Create trust before creating conversions.

By making people feel represented, the campaign reduced the psychological barrier of “this product is not for people like me” and reinforced Bokra’s core promise: everyone can begin building a stronger financial future, no matter how small the first step is.
