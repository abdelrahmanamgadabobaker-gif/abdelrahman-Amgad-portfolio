SOCIAL MEDIA & PRODUCT WORK

BOKRA SUKUK

Product awareness carousel.

A single two-slide carousel designed to communicate Bokra Sukuk’s product performance and encourage new users to begin saving through a Sharia-compliant product.

FORMAT Instagram Carousel · 2 Slides
OBJECTIVE Product Awareness & Saving Consideration
SLIDE 01

Performance proof

Highlighted that Bokra clients achieved a 22.1% return over the previous ten months, reinforcing credibility and demonstrating product performance.

SLIDE 02

Entry message

Invited new users to start saving through Bokra Sukuk with returns beginning from 18.1%, making the product feel accessible and attractive.
